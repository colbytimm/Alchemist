# Third-party notices

Alchemist's built-in color themes include palettes adapted from VS Code themes.
The theme files are embedded in the binary, so their notices ship with it.
Only each theme's colors were taken, mapped to Alchemist's roles.

| Theme | Adapted from | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `jarvis-hud` | [JARVIS HUD](https://github.com/AvinashReddy3108/CodemosModern-Themes-Registry) | Mohammad Areeb Ahmad | MIT |
| `dracula-at-midnight` | [Dracula At Midnight](https://github.com/walcew/dracula-at-midnight) 3.0.0 | Wallacy Santos Ferreira, after Dracula Theme | MIT |
| `enchanted-grove-dark` | [Enchanted Grove Dark](https://github.com/ChrisMcKee1/mtech-pro-vscode-themes), M Tech Themes 0.14.7 | M Tech | MIT |
| `synthwave-84` | [SynthWave '84](https://github.com/robb0wen/synthwave-vscode) 0.1.20 | Robb Owen | MIT |

## Themes not included

Two more themes were considered and left out, since nothing allows their
palettes to be shipped. MIT-licensed themes in the same spirit ship instead.

| Theme | Author | Why it is not included | Shipped instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arcane Theme 1.3.2 | Papa Ours | Its license asks for the author's permission before the palette is included in another distributed project. | `synthwave-84`, neon on a dark background |
| Ray Dark | Rayrarpa | No license and no source repository were found, so nothing grants redistribution. | `red`, an original Alchemist theme on a deep maroon background |
